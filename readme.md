# 📊 Retail Rescue: Executive Business Analytics Case Study

> An end-to-end Business Intelligence & Data Analytics project built using SQL and Power BI to solve real-world business problems for a fictional e-commerce company, **NovaMart**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

NovaMart, a fictional e-commerce retailer, has experienced significant sales growth but is facing challenges related to customer retention, product performance, and operational efficiency.

As a Business Data Analyst, the objective of this project is to analyze transactional data, identify business opportunities, and provide actionable recommendations to executives through data-driven insights.

This project follows an industry-style analytics workflow, covering everything from data quality assessment to executive reporting.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Validate data quality and integrity before analysis.
- Analyze revenue and sales performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Measure customer lifetime value and retention.
- Build executive KPIs and interactive dashboards.
- Deliver business recommendations to improve profitability and customer satisfaction.

## ✅ 3. Data Quality Assessment

Performed comprehensive data profiling including:

- Row count validation
- Missing value analysis
- Duplicate detection
- Primary key validation
- Referential integrity validation across multiple tables

### Key Findings

- ✅ No duplicate primary keys
- ✅ No orphan records
- ✅ High-quality relational dataset
- ✅ Reliable data for downstream analytics

---

## ✅ 4. Revenue Analysis

Business questions answered:

- Total Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by State

### Key Insights

- Health & Beauty is the highest revenue-generating category.
- São Paulo contributes the largest share of revenue.
- Average Order Value established as an executive KPI.

---

## ✅ 5. Product Analysis

Business questions answered:

- Highest revenue-generating categories
- Highest sales volume categories
- Average selling price by category
- Revenue contribution by category

### Key Insights

- Health & Beauty generates the highest revenue.
- Bed Bath & Table sells the highest number of products.
- Computers represent a premium, high-value category.
- Product portfolio analysis identifies opportunities for pricing and marketing optimization.

---

## ✅ 6. Customer Analytics

Business questions answered:

- Unique customers
- Repeat purchase rate
- Top customers by spending
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer value by state

### Key Insights

- 96,096 unique customers analyzed.
- Repeat Purchase Rate: **3.12%**
- Average Customer Lifetime Value: **R$141.62**
- High-value customers are primarily one-time purchasers, indicating opportunities to improve customer retention.
