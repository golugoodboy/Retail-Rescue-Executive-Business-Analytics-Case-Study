# Retail Rescue
## Executive Business Analytics Case Study

## Executive Summary

NovaMart is a rapidly growing e-commerce retailer experiencing increasing sales but declining business performance.

Although customer orders continue to grow, leadership has observed:

- increasing delivery delays
- declining customer satisfaction
- inconsistent seller performance
- uncertainty regarding business growth drivers

The executive team requires a complete business analysis to identify root causes and recommend strategic actions to improve operational efficiency, customer experience, and long-term profitability.


## Business Objectives

The objective of this project is to investigate the operational health of NovaMart by analyzing customer behavior, order performance, seller efficiency, logistics, and product trends.

The analysis aims to answer critical business questions and provide actionable recommendations for executive leadership.

## Assumptions

Missing values are assumed to be random unless evidence suggests otherwise.

Revenue is calculated using product price and freight values.

Historical data reflects normal business operations.
